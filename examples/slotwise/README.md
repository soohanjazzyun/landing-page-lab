# Slotwise

This is a fictional product brief for testing Landing Page Story.

## Product facts

- Audience: a solo consultant scheduling individual sessions with clients.
- Set weekly availability.
- Share a booking link.
- Clients choose an available slot.
- Times display in the viewer's time zone.
- Email confirmations follow a booking.
- The intended next action is `Preview a booking page`.

## Current copy to improve

Scheduling reimagined for a connected world. Slotwise is an innovative,
end-to-end experience that enables professionals to optimize their time
through a holistic suite of scheduling capabilities.

## Boundaries and unknowns

- No payment collection, calendar synchronization, group bookings, or reminders.
- No testimonials, user counts, conversion uplift, or financial-savings claims.
- Pricing and product availability are unspecified.
- No live Slotwise website is provided; the CTA describes a proposed action.

## Request

Create an approachable English landing-page outline and copy. Respect a
minimal, existing visual design. Do not invent an offer or claim the product
is free. Include a visual of available slots and a timezone label, then a
Framer-ready section-by-section handoff without publishing anything.
