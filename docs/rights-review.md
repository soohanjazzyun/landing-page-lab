## What changed and what remains

The story skill is still informed by Donald Miller's approach: a reader seeks progress, encounters a real obstacle, receives credible help, takes a real action, and can understand the resulting change. These are expressed as original operational judgments rather than an official worksheet or fixed set of page fields. Resonance and offer likewise apply broad ideas through original decisions, evidence handling, examples, and implementation boundaries. All three are independently authored adaptations; none is an officially approved implementation.

The current skill folders contain no official PDF, book chapter, diagram, logo, copied worksheet, course transcript, or third-party skill file. Attribution links identify inspiration; they do not supply a license or imply endorsement. The skills do not require future agents to retrieve framework source materials. Product evidence supplied for a user's task is a separate matter and must be used within its actual permissions.

No comprehensive similarity comparison against complete books or paid programs was performed. No author or rights holder was contacted, no written permission was obtained, and no conclusion was reached about contract formation, enforceability, governing law, or the reach of the StoryBrand AI/competition terms or Acquisition restrictions. This record does not guarantee noninfringement. Removing source links or changing names would not itself cure a past contractual problem. If a particular distribution or reuse depends on restricted content, its permission or other legal basis still needs to be established, or that material replaced.
