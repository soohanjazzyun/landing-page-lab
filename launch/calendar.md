## The day-14 decision

- **No completed external run:** observe the next willing user before expanding distribution. Identify the first blocked step. Do not mistake impressions for use.
- **Completed runs, recurring errors:** ship one focused fix with the failing brief and corrected behavior. A generic rebrand is not the next experiment.
- **Useful outputs, little reach:** use a consented case in the result-first comparison format, then choose one appropriate public surface from [prospects](prospects.md).
- **Useful outputs and independent shares:** repeat the strongest case format, at a sustainable cadence. Ask permission for the exact next case.

The thresholds above are qualitative operating rules, not statistical benchmarks. Low sample sizes cannot prove a winning message. Record conflicting signals rather than selecting only positive feedback.

## Days 15-30

Publish at most two useful cases per week, run one agreed complementary workflow, and make one substantive release from recurring failures. Reuse the strongest case as a technical write-up. Creator outreach and follow-ups are excluded by the current no-contact direction. Review real use and time per case before adding another channel.
