## Story

**Headline**

Turn your SaaS onboarding flow into a concrete design.

**Supporting paragraph**

Mina Studio reviews one existing SaaS onboarding flow around the first-use task you choose, then delivers annotated Figma screens and a clickable prototype for up to five screens. Your developer implements the design. Explore an illustrative sample below.

**Primary CTA**

[See a sample project](#sample-project)

**Reason / assumption, outside the copy:** The current hero does not explain what the service actually does. This version connects the founder’s chosen task, the review, the design artifacts, and the developer’s next step. It uses the documented mechanism without promising activation, retention, or other business results.

## Resonance

**Headline**

For the SaaS you’ve built, start with first use.

**Supporting paragraph**

You have an existing product and a first-use task to focus on. Mina Studio works with solo founders and small founder-led teams on that one flow, up to five screens. Bring your product link or walkthrough; receive annotated Figma screens and a clickable prototype for your developer. The sample below shows an illustrative project.

**Primary CTA**

[See a sample project](#sample-project)

**Reason / assumption, outside the copy:** This opens with the reader’s product stage and a bounded task rather than a general design promise. The working hypothesis is that the intended founder recognizes the value of focusing on one flow while their developer retains implementation. That preference has not been validated; this service would not meet a reader’s need for an entire product redesign or implementation.

## Offer

**Headline**

One onboarding flow. A defined design handoff.

**Supporting paragraph**

Get annotated Figma screens and a clickable prototype for one existing onboarding or first-use flow, up to five screens, plus one handoff call and one revision. Supply a product link or walkthrough recording, your chosen first-use task, and existing design files where available. Your developer handles implementation. Preview an illustrative project below.

**Primary CTA**

[See a sample project](#sample-project)

**Reason / assumption, outside the copy:** The main uncertainty is what this design engagement includes and what the founder must contribute. This version makes the deliverables, limits, inputs, and implementation responsibility inspectable. The action remains a sample preview; it makes no offer of a consultation, price, deadline, or guarantee.

## Strongest direction for this source

**Offer is the strongest recommendation.** The brief supplies unusually concrete deliverables and boundaries but no customer proof or validated audience language. Leading with the defined handoff puts that evidence to work and makes the illustrative sample a natural next step. Story is the clearest compact explanation of the process; Resonance adds a plausible existing-product situation whose relevance still needs checking with intended readers.

This is an editorial judgment, not a measured conversion result. A useful next check would ask intended readers what they would receive, what they would supply, who implements the work, and what the button opens. No reader testing, visual inspection, or destination verification was performed in this copy-only run.
