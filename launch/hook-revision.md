# Stronger launch hooks

Prepared 2026-11-03 for Landing Page Lab by Jazz. The recommended hook and complete post were [published with the 34-second film](https://x.com/Jazz_from_Korea/status/2117600499033928157). Other candidates remain drafts.

## Recommendation

**Your AI put a gradient on your README.**

Use this as the post's first line and the video's first frame. Show the weak pitch immediately, then change the words while the page design stays fixed. The joke names the problem; the comparison demonstrates what the skills work on.

## Ranked top three

### 1. Your AI put a gradient on your README.

**Attention:** The line lands as a roast before it reads as a product announcement. "Gradient" and "README" create an image before the reader reaches the product description.

**Specificity:** It speaks directly to founders who use coding agents. It identifies the visible habit and the underlying source material.

**Proof fit:** The demo keeps facts and design fixed and changes the pitch. Show the original abstract copy, then the three authored directions. No conversion claim is needed to make the change visible.

**Tradeoff:** Use a real source pane and the existing page in the opening. Do not present the authored demo as an automatic README-to-website generation run.

**Full launch post, 203 weighted characters:**

```text
Your AI put a gradient on your README.

I built Landing Page Lab: 3 open-source agent skills for the story, audience, and offer.

Watch the pitch change. Same facts. Same design.

https://github.com/soohanjazzyun/landing-page-lab
```

### 2. If your landing page needs you standing next to it, it's still a draft.

**Attention:** A founder can immediately recognize the experience of explaining a page aloud. "Still a draft" gives that experience a sharp verdict.

**Specificity:** It targets the page's ability to explain the product by itself. It avoids a broad claim that every AI-built page is bad.

**Proof fit:** Replace a generic hero with a concrete input, output, buyer, or next step. The comparison makes the editorial decision inspectable, although it does not prove visitor comprehension.

**Tradeoff:** The first frame needs large type and a quick page reveal because this hook is longer than the first choice.

**Full launch post, 227 weighted characters:**

```text
If your landing page needs you standing next to it, it's still a draft.

Landing Page Lab gives your agent 3 ways to fix the pitch: story, audience, offer.

Same product facts. Same design. Open source.

https://github.com/soohanjazzyun/landing-page-lab
```

### 3. Your landing page has a hero section. Your customer is nowhere in it.

**Attention:** The second sentence turns a familiar UI term into an accusation. The wordplay also carries the substantive point.

**Specificity:** It names an actual section and a missing person. That gives the video a precise place to start.

**Proof fit:** Start on RelayNote's abstract introduction and switch to its solo-founder audience direction. The unchanged visual shell keeps attention on whose task the words describe.

**Tradeoff:** This hook naturally favors the Story and Audience perspectives. Reveal Offer soon afterward so the video presents all three skills.

**Full launch post, 228 weighted characters:**

```text
Your landing page has a hero section.
Your customer is nowhere in it.

Landing Page Lab: 3 open-source agent skills for the story, audience, and offer.

One product. Three pitches. Same facts and design.

https://github.com/soohanjazzyun/landing-page-lab
```

Counts include line breaks. All post text is ASCII; the GitHub URL counts as 23 characters. The counts leave room for a small edit without relying on a full 280 characters.

## Three video openings

These are alternative openings. Use one, then continue into the same comparison. Keep the product page readable and use a persistent, unobtrusive "Fictional product / authored demo" label during example footage.

### A. The README roast, recommended

| Time | Exact on-screen text | Visible action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0-1.1s | **YOUR AI PUT A GRADIENT** | Start with the page already visible. Large type arrives on the first frame; no logo introduction. |
| 1.1-2.1s | **ON YOUR README.** | Reveal a narrow source pane beside the page. Highlight a real abstract phrase from the source and its corresponding before-copy. |
| 2.1-3.0s | **FIX THE PITCH.** | Remove the source pane and switch the existing page from Before to Story. Keep the frame and layout locked. |

At 3s, introduce "Story / Audience / Offer" and show the actual authored directions with the same facts and design. Avoid implying a live model run or typing an invented installation result.

### B. The founder still has to explain it

| Time | Exact on-screen text | Visible action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0-1.0s | **THE PAGE IS LIVE.** | Show the existing Before hero. |
| 1.0-2.0s | **YOU'RE STILL EXPLAINING IT.** | Put a plain annotation beside the abstract headline: "It organizes customer-call notes." This is an editorial annotation, not a fabricated customer quote. |
| 2.0-3.0s | **PUT THAT ON THE PAGE.** | Switch to the Story direction and briefly highlight its concrete product action. |

At 3s, reveal the product name and the three skills. The audience and offer changes then show why a useful pitch involves more than deleting jargon.

### C. The missing customer

| Time | Exact on-screen text | Visible action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0-1.0s | **HERO SECTION: FOUND.** | A crisp outline frames the actual Before hero. |
| 1.0-2.0s | **CUSTOMER: MISSING.** | Highlight the source's intended audience beside the generic hero. No fake analytics or visitor reaction. |
| 2.0-3.0s | **WRITE FOR SOMEONE.** | Switch to RelayNote's Audience direction and emphasize its named audience and task. |

At 3s, bring in Story and Offer as the other two decisions. Show the product name after the opening payoff.

## Credits and evidence reply

The main post can stay focused. Use the following factual reply alongside the video and repository credits:

```text
Original skills by Jazz, informed by Donald Miller, Seth Godin, and Alex Hormozi. Independent project; no affiliation or endorsement.

Demo: 3 fictional products, 9 authored comparisons. Actual agent trials and limitations are in the repo.
```

The authored comparisons demonstrate copy decisions. The independent runs document actual skill use and its limitations. Neither supports a conversion-lift claim or a promise of GitHub growth.
