# RelayNote

This is a fictional product brief for testing Landing Page Story.

## Product facts

- Audience: a solo SaaS founder who has interviewed potential customers and
  needs to revisit what they actually said before choosing the next feature.
- Import interview notes as `.txt` or `.md` files.
- Group saved excerpts by topic.
- Every saved excerpt links back to its original note.
- Export grouped notes as Markdown.
- A sample workspace is the intended next step; use the CTA label
  `Explore a sample workspace` in page drafts.

## Current copy to improve

RelayNote is a next-generation customer intelligence workspace. Leverage a
unified platform to unlock actionable insights and streamline your discovery
workflow. Our powerful capabilities empower ambitious builders to innovate.

## Boundaries and unknowns

- No audio upload, audio transcription, AI-generated insights, or integrations.
- No customer counts, testimonials, measured time savings, or retention data.
- Pricing, hosting details, and data-security claims are not specified.
- There is no real RelayNote service at an external URL. The sample-workspace
  CTA is a proposed action in an illustrative page, not a live service link.

## Request

Write an English landing-page plan and copy from these facts. Keep the
tone practical and calm. Show the product itself, including an excerpt and
its source, in the hero visual. End with a self-contained HTML/CSS build
prompt, but do not build or publish the page unless asked.
