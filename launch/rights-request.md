# Bounded source/rights clarification draft

Not sent. This is an optional concrete request when review of the actual materials indicates that source permission or contractual clarification is needed. It is not a statement that all marketing ideas require permission. The [rights record](../docs/rights-review.md) distinguishes original expression, actual source materials, branding, and unresolved application of current terms. Credits alone do not authorize copying.

Use a rightsholder's verified official contact route. Do not infer an address. Attach only the actual small excerpts/files under discussion, not a whole book or downloaded source collection. Name the exact source, retrieval date, use, and license. A generic supportive reply is not permission for every future use.

## English draft

Hello [rights/licensing team],

I'm Jazz, author of Landing Page Lab, an independent open-source project that helps founders prepare landing-page briefs with coding agents.

I would like to clarify a bounded use of [exact named source and URL]. The proposed public material is [exact files/excerpts and revision], attached for review. It consists of original instructions and examples, with a source-credit link. We do not propose to distribute your book, worksheets, diagrams, website copy, logos, or service-generated output, or claim certification, affiliation, or endorsement.

The intended distribution is a free public GitHub repository under the MIT license for our original material, with attribution that does not purport to license any third-party rights. The source could be used by people through their own coding agents; it is not a model-training dataset or a hosted version of your service.

We noticed [exact clause / material-specific restriction from the current terms]. Could you clarify whether you consider that restriction applicable to the attached original implementation? If written permission is needed for the specific proposed use, please let us know the scope, required attribution, exclusions, and any conditions. We will not describe silence as approval or commission paid work without agreeing on it first.

Project: https://github.com/soohanjazzyun/landing-page-lab

Thank you,
Jazz
