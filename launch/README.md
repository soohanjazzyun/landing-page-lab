Three original agent skills tackle three different decisions:

- **Story:** make the product understandable through a customer task, explanation, page sequence, and next step.
- **Resonance:** choose the people the page is for, their situation, language, and meaningful tradeoffs.
- **Offer:** make existing deliverables, value, first-use effort, terms, and commitment easier to evaluate.

## Publication discipline

Use live links only after they resolve. Keep fictional/authored labels on every excerpt. A local prompt composer prepares a task; it does not call an AI model. Only describe agent compatibility that has actually been tested. No conversion lift, customer count, endorsement, publication, message response, or star gain is established by creating this packet.

No purchase, star, repost, subscription, or positive review is required to use the files or submit a case. Do not manufacture installs, invite coordinated votes, use a creator's issue tracker for unrelated promotion, or send repeated follow-ups. The 3,000-star goal remains an objective until observed.
