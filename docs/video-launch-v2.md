# Launch film: Landing Page Lab

Delivered 2026-11-03. [34-second MP4](../assets/landing-page-lab-launch-v2.mp4) and [poster](../assets/launch-v2-poster.png).

Opening line: **Your AI put a gradient on your README.**

The square film uses oversized type, an enlarged actual page comparison, causal cursor clicks, a page-to-mosaic match, a real implementation handoff, and a performed install/end card. Story, Audience, and Offer hold the same RelayNote source facts and visual shell constant. Example footage remains labeled fictional/authored; the film does not show a model generating those examples live or claim a conversion improvement.

## Reference and originality

The visual reference was [Che0dan's public launch video](https://x.com/Che0dan/status/2117558469909717046). Its visible media metadata was 1080×1080 and about 36.54 seconds. Observed frames used a bright stage, large black typography, a finished output, cropped real product UI, and a simple performed end card. Those editorial devices informed this new film. Its source footage, music, title, product, and graphics were not reused.

All visible product screens come from this project's original authored examples and interface. The soundtrack and effects are an original deterministic local synthesis, without third-party music samples or paid generation. The editable HyperFrames project, system font, production captures, and rendering dependencies remain local under `video/launch-v2`; proprietary font files are not redistributed in the repository or release.
