# One Product, Three Pitches

A repeatable series by Jazz: hold the product facts and visual design constant, then inspect what Story, Resonance, and Offer change. These opening episodes are **fictional products with authored copy**. They are not customers, measured experiments, verbatim model runs, or proof of conversion lift. Each proposed CTA belongs to the fictional brief; it does not lead to a real service.

## Episode 1: RelayNote

[Source brief](../examples/relaynote/README.md). A solo SaaS founder imports written interview notes, groups excerpts by topic, follows each excerpt to its source, and exports Markdown. No transcription, AI insights, integrations, customer metrics, or specified price.

**Original opening:** “RelayNote is a next-generation customer intelligence workspace.”

| Pitch | Headline | Supporting copy | Same CTA | Decision exposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Story | Revisit what customers said before choosing your next feature. | Import your interview notes, group the useful excerpts, and return to the original note when you need the context. | Explore a sample workspace | Explain the founder's task before the feature list |
| Resonance | For solo founders whose next feature starts with an interview. | Work from the written notes you already have. Keep excerpts by topic and tied to their source, without adding a transcription workflow. | Explore a sample workspace | Choose a narrow working situation; avoid claiming every researcher is the buyer |
| Offer | Import notes. Group excerpts. Export the result. | Bring .txt or .md notes, keep the original source attached, and export grouped notes as Markdown. Inspect the proposed workflow in a sample workspace. | Explore a sample workspace | Make the included workflow explicit; leave price and setup effort unresolved |

**Still unknown:** pricing, hosting, setup time, security commitments, and whether the audience prefers this over its existing tools. No promise of time saved or better feature decisions.

## Episode 2: ShipWatch

[Source brief](../examples/shipwatch/README.md). A solo developer's scheduled job sends a heartbeat; a late heartbeat triggers an email alert; a history view records heartbeats. No recovery, retry, SMS, Slack, tracing, uptime promise, or specified price.

**Original opening:** “An advanced observability platform with intelligent workflow monitoring...”

| Pitch | Headline | Supporting copy | Same CTA | Decision exposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Story | See when a scheduled job misses its heartbeat. | Have your job send a heartbeat after it runs. ShipWatch flags a heartbeat later than your configured window and sends an email alert. | Set up a test heartbeat | Make the event and response concrete |
| Resonance | For the solo developer who is also on call for the cron jobs. | Check recorded heartbeats and get an email when one arrives late. This is a focused job-monitoring workflow, without tracing or automatic recovery. | Set up a test heartbeat | Name the operator and the relevant limits |
| Offer | Test one heartbeat before wiring up the rest. | Configure a window, send a heartbeat from your job, and inspect its run history. Late heartbeats are flagged with an email alert. | Set up a test heartbeat | Show the first commitment and included behavior; do not invent an endpoint or command |

**Still unknown:** price, delivery latency, retention, hosting, and exact setup instructions. “Test one heartbeat” is a suggested first-use sequence, not a measured completion time or a free-tier claim.

## Episode 3: Slotwise

[Source brief](../examples/slotwise/README.md). A solo consultant sets weekly availability, shares a link, and lets a client select a slot in the viewer's time zone; email confirmations follow. No payments, calendar sync, group bookings, reminders, specified price, or live service.

**Original opening:** “Scheduling reimagined for a connected world.”

| Pitch | Headline | Supporting copy | Same CTA | Decision exposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Story | Let clients choose a time from your available slots. | Set weekly availability and share your booking link. Email confirmations follow a booking. | Preview a booking page | Explain the actual scheduling sequence |
| Resonance | A booking page for consultants who work one client at a time. | Share the times you offer for individual sessions. Clients see slots in their own time zone and choose an available time. | Preview a booking page | Choose solo, individual-session work; do not imply a team or group-booking tool |
| Offer | Show clients your availability before they choose a session. | The page displays your weekly slots in the viewer's time zone. A booking triggers email confirmations. Preview the proposed page before setting up your own. | Preview a booking page | Make the experience and limits inspectable without inventing terms |

**Still unknown:** pricing, release availability, cancellation policy, privacy commitments, and setup effort. Do not call it free or promise a calendar integration.

## Make the comparison useful

Use the same viewport, type, colors, layout, CTA, illustration, and factual ledger for each pitch. If an audience or sequence changes, label it as a messaging choice. Do not give one variant a polished mockup and another a wall of text. A reader's preference is feedback; it is not a conversion measurement.

For a real case, attach: the owner's exact consent; source revision and access date; original copy; all three raw skill outputs; displayed edited copy; agent/model and skill version; manual edits; the founder's corrections; unresolved evidence; and a reproduction prompt. Keep criticism and disagreement alongside positive feedback.

## Case-page skeleton

```text

Status: fictional/authored OR real owner-consented case
Source, date, skill version, agent/model, exact prompt
Unchanged facts / unknowns / primary CTA / visual constraints

Story pitch + one consequential decision
Resonance pitch + audience choice and exclusions
Offer pitch + included terms and undecided founder choices
What we edited manually
What the owner corrected / what remains uncertain
Reproduce the workflow
Optional owner-approved response (never required)
```
