# Mina Studio: a recorded skill comparison

Prepared 2026-11-04. [Open the interactive case](https://soohanjazzyun.github.io/landing-page-lab/cases/mina-studio/) · [14-second film](../assets/mina-studio-proof.mp4).

## What is actual

A fresh evaluator without the authoring conversation received the fictional
[source brief](evaluation/mina-demo/source.md), the [task](evaluation/mina-demo/task.md),
and the three skill files with relevant references. It wrote the first completed
[output](evaluation/mina-demo/output.md) directly and kept a [read/action record](evaluation/mina-demo/run-notes.md).
The page reproduces each headline and supporting paragraph verbatim; it does
not stage a live model response. The Before text comes from the source.

The product-design service and persona are fictional. The [portrait](mina-portrait.md)
was generated separately with the host image tool. No real client, endorsement,
delivered design engagement, conversion improvement, or paid copywriter replacement
is claimed. The picture is the same across all four views.

## Reproduce and inspect

1. Open the case and switch Before, Story, Resonance, and Offer. Query links
   such as `?lens=story` preserve a direction, including browser history.
2. Follow the sample CTA to inspect the documented scope. It is an illustrative
   scope, not a booking form or a delivered client Figma file.
3. Download the source, exact task, raw output, and run notes from the case.
4. From a full Git checkout, run `npm run verify:cases`. It checks 23 recorded
   byte/version/public-export assertions for this case and the separate
   [actual own-page audit](evaluation/current-page/output.md).
5. For another writing run, use the skill instructions at the recorded commit
   and the saved source/task in a fresh agent. LLM output may differ. The
   manifests do not invent an unavailable model identity or prove effectiveness.

## Film production and verification

The film is 14.000 seconds: 420 frames at 1080×1080 and 30 fps, H.264/yuv420p
with AAC 48 kHz stereo audio. It opens on the full Offer result and the portrait
at frame zero, then shows the Before text and real lens-control captures,
source/output provenance, and the install command. All case screens are
captures of the implemented page; the cursor and transitions are editorial motion.

The soundtrack is a 14-second edit of the project's original procedurally
created score from the earlier launch film. No reference footage or music
was reused. Measured delivery audio is −14.9 LUFS and −1.4 dBTP, with zero
detected clipping. The sound was measured, not subjectively listened to.

HyperFrames' final lint/runtime/layout/motion/contrast/craft/audio gates passed
with no errors or warnings. Informational findings cover the intended surface
wipes. Decoded first/last frames, lens changes, phone-scale views, and transition
seams were inspected. The editable production is local under `video/face-proof/`,
which is excluded from public source; only rendered media is included.

The film and portrait are a creative hypothesis about attention. No click,
install, star, or conversion gain is established by producing them. See the
[distribution hypothesis and prepared post](../launch/face-led-case.md).
