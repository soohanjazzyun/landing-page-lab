# Landing Page Lab launch desk

Prepared for Jazz on 2026-11-03. Public language: English. Canonical target: [soohanjazzyun/landing-page-lab](https://github.com/soohanjazzyun/landing-page-lab). Demo target: [GitHub Pages](https://soohanjazzyun.github.io/landing-page-lab/). These are canonical destinations, not a claim that publication has completed. Check [release status](../docs/release.md).

**The pitch:** Your AI can build a page. Give it a better brief.

Three original agent skills tackle three different decisions:

- **Story:** make the product understandable through a customer task, explanation, page sequence, and next step.
- **Resonance:** choose the people the page is for, their situation, language, and meaningful tradeoffs.
- **Offer:** make existing deliverables, value, first-use effort, terms, and commitment easier to evaluate.

Jazz authored the implementation. Donald Miller, Seth Godin, and Alex Hormozi are credited inspirations, not collaborators or endorsers. The source/rights record is in [references](../docs/references.md) and [rights review](../docs/rights-review.md). Credit is not permission to copy third-party materials.

## Start here

1. Complete the factual [release checklist](../docs/release.md), including the three install routes and live-link checks.
2. Record the [27-second comparison](demo-shotlist.md) against the actual release.
3. Choose one of the [three launch hooks](channel-copy.md). The confirmed X target is `@jazz_from_korea`; post only from the actual account and stay available for replies. This packet records no completed post.
4. Work through the [14-day calendar](calendar.md). Its posts are drafts, not scheduled or submitted activity.
5. Run the [Founder Clinic](homepage-clinic.md) and get [specific case consent](owner-consent.md) before publishing someone else's product.
6. Approach relevant people with [tailored pitches](collaboration-pitches.md), using the [eight verified prospects and surfaces](prospects.md). No guessed email addresses or bulk outreach.
7. Record observations using [measurement definitions](measurement.md), [metrics.csv](metrics.csv), and [activity.csv](activity.csv). Keep [hypotheses](hypotheses.csv) separate.

## Publication discipline

Use live links only after they resolve. Keep fictional/authored labels on every excerpt. A local prompt composer prepares a task; it does not call an AI model. Only describe agent compatibility that has actually been tested. No conversion lift, customer count, endorsement, publication, message response, or star gain is established by creating this packet.

No purchase, star, repost, subscription, or positive review is required to use the files or submit a case. Do not manufacture installs, invite coordinated votes, use a creator's issue tracker for unrelated promotion, or send repeated follow-ups. The 3,000-star goal remains an objective until observed.
